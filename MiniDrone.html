SPARROE: Low-Cost Quadcopter

Summer 2024 · Biola University
Component Selection and Assembly Electrical Architecture

Overview

Five of us built SPARROE as a $56 alternative to the $180 drones on the market. Over six weeks during my freshman year, we completed a team build spanning chassis, electronics, and software. I selected the motor driver boards, battery, and boost module, planned the electrical architecture, and soldered and assembled the flight electronics onto the frame.

Design Reflection

If I were approaching this same project as a senior, I'd perform a more rigorous power and thrust analysis before selecting components. Although the batteries provided sufficient nominal capacity, the 1S voltage and voltage losses through the L298N motor drivers limited power available to the motors under load. I'd select motors, drivers, and battery as a complete system using measured current and thrust requirements, with margin for peak loads.
