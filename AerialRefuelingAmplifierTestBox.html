Test Box for Characterizing an Aerial Refueling Amplifier

Summer 2026 · Palomar Products
Electrical Component Selection Signal Characterization

Overview

Palomar needed to understand the electrical behavior and interface requirements of an externally sourced Aerial Refueling Amplifier (ARA) before integrating it into its system. I developed a test setup based on the functional test schematic in MIL-DTL-38449D, sourced the required components, created the wiring diagram, and worked with a technician to assemble the physical test box.

Approach

I used the test box to exercise the amplifier's controls and check its behavior against the applicable functional test requirements. I then used oscilloscopes, waveform generators, digital multimeters, and an Audio Precision analyzer to examine signal levels and response. This testing also helped clarify interface behaviors that were not fully specified in the documentation.

Test Box Circuit Diagram

Results

The test results helped establish hardware interface requirements for integrating the amplifier into Palomar's system and informed preliminary interface designs. This work gave me experience translating a functional test specification into a physical test setup and using laboratory measurements to support hardware integration.
